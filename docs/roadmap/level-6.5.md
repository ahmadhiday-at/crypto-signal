# 🧱 Level 6.5 — Signal Quality (proven edge)

Roadmap level: goals, milestones and success criteria. Overview: [README.md](./README.md).
**Objective:** turn the signal into one that measurably helps: fix the weaknesses Level 5–6 found, validate every change out-of-sample, and show honestly how good the signal is. Gate for Level 7.

**Diagnosis (backtest BTC/SOL/BNB, 1 year, see [level-6.md](./level-6.md)):**
1. **Counter-trend signals:** the score ignores the 1d/4h trend, so BUYs fire in a downtrend (BUY is always the worst side; SOL 35 of 50 taken trades were BUY in a falling year).
2. **Targets too close:** TP1 = nearest opposite zone; ±90% of candidates are rejected by `low_rr` (median plan RR 0.34).
3. **Score 5 is too easy:** support + momentum alone reach it; |score| 5 → −0.35 R, ≥ 6 ≈ break-even.
4. **Tight 15m stops vs costs:** median risk 1–1.9% of price, so a 0.1% fee costs ±0.1 R per trade.
5. **Wrong direction, not noise:** median LOSS after 18–20 entry candles; only ±15% hit SL within 1 hour.
6. **The dashboard shows BUY/SELL as if usable** while the measured expectancy is negative.

## Milestones (in order)

**A. Honest output**
- [x] **Edge indicator on the dashboard (v2.12.0):** `GET /api/edge/:symbol`; next to the signal, the latest backtest result for the symbol (taken expectancy, win rate, trades, fee, date) and live Signal Log stats (Level 4); label the signal "unproven" / "negative edge" when expectancy ≤ 0 or the sample is too small.

**B. Signal logic** (each change is a sweepable backtest option first; live defaults change only after step B4)
- [x] **B1. HTF trend filter (v2.16.0):** per-TF `trend` (`up`/`down`/`ranging`, MA14 vs MA50) in the Market State; filter `against_htf_trend` rejects a BUY when a checked TF trends down and a SELL when one trends up. Config `filters.htf_trend` = `off` (default) / `htf` / `htf+structure`; sweep key `htf_trend`. SELL is not limited to down trends (the roadmap draft): SELL into an up trend was break-even or better on BTC/BNB.
  - **Result (2026-11-02, 1 year, fee 0.1%, split 0.7, defaults otherwise), taken exp R off / htf / htf+structure:** BTC −0.244 (71) / −0.204 (34) / −0.152 (19); SOL −0.284 (50) / −0.551 (26) / −0.915 (7); BNB −0.212 (67) / −0.221 (34) / −0.332 (19). Out-of-sample: BTC worse with the filter (−0.48 R, 8 trades), BNB better (+0.17 R, 7 trades), SOL worse; samples too small.
  - **Verdict:** halves the trades and losses on BTC, but no edge and not consistent across pairs → stays `off`. The clearest pattern is BUY with 1d and 4h both down: −0.53 R (BTC, 14), −0.53 R (SOL, 14), −0.69 R (BNB, 4); to be rechecked on 2–3 years (B4) before a BUY-only veto is considered. The main loss source remains the win rate (13–20%): next B2.
- [x] **B2. Target and entry location (v2.17.0):** config `signal.target` = `tp1` (nearest zone, default) / `rr_zone` (first zone ahead with RR ≥ `min_rr`) / `tp2`; `plan.entry_distance_atr` and filter `far_from_entry` with config `filters.entry_max_atr` (0 = off, default); sweep keys `target=rr_zone`, `entry_max_atr`.
  - **Result (2026-11-02, 1 year, fee 0.1%, split 0.7, defaults otherwise), taken exp R tp1 → rr_zone:** BTC −0.244 (71) → −0.041 (336); SOL −0.284 (50) → −0.034 (261); BNB −0.212 (67) → −0.072 (339). Out-of-sample rr_zone: BTC −0.024 (112), SOL +0.054 (90), BNB −0.197 (105).
  - **Why it looks better:** with rr_zone 60–64% of trades EXPIRE (96 entry candles) before TP1 or SL; TP1 win rate is only 5–7%. Losses shrink because trades time out near entry, not because the target is reached → no edge yet, defaults unchanged.
  - **`entry_max_atr` 1–2 changes almost nothing:** taken trades are already in or next to the entry zone (`no_mans_land` and the score's `near_support`/`near_resistance` points).
  - **Open:** the 96-candle expiry is fixed in code; a target farther away needs a longer holding time (or a trailing exit) to be judged. Recheck on 2–3 years (B4).
- [ ] **B3. Score calibration:** reason weights from the breakdown (which reasons raise win rate / expectancy), review the threshold. Since v2.18.0 the points are config `scoring` and every other calculation value (indicators, zones, market thresholds, divergence, expiry) is in `config/calculation.json` too; since v2.19.0 each can be swept (`--sweep scoring.near_zone=1,2,3`).
  - **First sweep (2026-11-02, 1 year, fee 0.1%, split 0.7):** `scoring.near_zone` × `momentum` × `htf_zone` = 1–3 × 1–3 × 2–4 (27 sets). Lowering `near_zone` to 1 cuts the trades and the loss on BTC/BNB (best: near_zone 1, momentum 2, htf_zone 2 → BTC +0.10 R and BNB +0.13 R, but only 15 and 18 trades; SOL −0.77 R, 8 trades). The out-of-sample pick (≥ 30 in-sample trades) stays negative on BTC (−0.16 R) and BNB (−0.27 R), SOL +0.02 R. No point set is positive on all three pairs with enough trades → defaults unchanged; recheck on 2–3 years (B4).
- [ ] **Expiry check (v2.19.0 sweep `evaluation.expiry_candles=96,192,384`):** a longer holding time raises the TP1 win rate (BTC rr_zone 6 → 20%) but not the expectancy (BTC rr_zone −0.04 → −0.09 R, SOL −0.03 → −0.05 R, BNB −0.07 → −0.04 R; tp1 worse on BTC/SOL, better on BNB in-sample only). Expiry is not the missing edge; stays 96.
- [x] **B4. Robust validation (2026-11-02, v2.19.0):** 3 years (2023-11 → 2026-11), BTC/SOL/BNB, fee 0.1%, `--split 0.7` (out-of-sample = 2025-12-08 → 2026-11), defaults otherwise. **Success criterion not met by any variant.**
  - **15m entry, 8 variants** (`target` tp1/rr_zone × `htf_trend` off/htf+structure × `scoring.near_zone` 1/2), taken exp R live → best: BTC −0.055 (208) → +0.152 (64, tp1 + htf+structure); SOL −0.320 (136) → −0.020 (221, rr_zone + htf+structure); BNB −0.051 (210) → +0.033 (118, tp1 + near_zone 1). Out-of-sample of the in-sample pick: BTC −0.140 (18), SOL −0.027 (74), BNB −0.132 (34). Every variant is negative out-of-sample on at least two pairs.
  - **By year (live defaults):** BTC +0.48 R (2023, 13 trades), −0.13 (2024), +0.03 (2025), −0.22 (2026); BNB and SOL similar or worse. The edge is not stable over time; 2026 is the worst year everywhere.
  - **Fees decide it:** before fees BTC +0.050 R and BNB +0.041 R per trade, after fees −0.055 / −0.051. A 0.1% round trip costs 0.09–0.11 R because the 15m stop is only ±1.2% of price (SOL 2.2%, −0.27 R before fees).
  - **1h entry** (env roles changed for the backtest only): layout TF_ENTRY 1h / MID 4h / STRUCTURE 1d / HTF 1w with rr_zone + htf+structure → BTC +0.505 R (43 trades; out-of-sample +0.59 R, 8 trades) but SOL −0.109 (19) and BNB −0.133 (31). Layout entry 1h / mid 1h / 4h / 1d: all pairs ≤ 0 except BNB rr_zone +0.007 (400). Not consistent across pairs.
  - **Conclusion:** the S/R-bounce score cannot be tuned into an edge that holds on BTC, SOL and BNB after fees. Live defaults stay; the dashboard keeps the signal labelled negative / unproven. Options for a next level: (1) a different signal model (e.g. trend-following / breakout instead of bounce), (2) higher entry timeframes with fewer, larger trades (BTC 1h/1w is the only lead, small sample), (3) per-pair parameters instead of one shared set (would change the success criterion), (4) lower costs (maker fees). Level 7 stays blocked.
- [x] **B5. New signal models (v2.20.0):** config `signal.model` = `bounce` (default) / `trend_pullback` / `breakout` ([market-state.md §4.2](../contract/market-state.md#42-trend-models-added-in-2200)), config group `trend`, sweep key `model`. 3 years, fee 0.1%, split 0.7 (out-of-sample 2025-12-08 → 2026-11):
  - **15m entry (live layout):** every trend variant is worse than bounce (−0.03 to −0.27 R, 1,100–4,300 trades); too many small trades for the fee.
  - **1h entry, roles 1h / 1h / 4h / 1d:** best around 0 (BTC breakout 3 ATR / 3R +0.006, SOL −0.013, BNB +0.026); out-of-sample negative on all three.
  - **1h entry, roles 1h / 4h / 1d / 1w, `trend_pullback`, `trend.stop_atr` 3, `trend.target_r` 2 (closest to the criterion):** BTC +0.078 R (452 trades), BNB +0.040 (415), SOL −0.123 (323); out-of-sample BTC +0.059 (119), SOL +0.007 (132), BNB −0.005 (117). A refinement sweep (stop 2.5–5 ATR × target 1.5–3R, 12 sets) finds no set positive out-of-sample on all three: BNB stays ≤ −0.005, SOL is negative in-sample for every set.
  - **Verdict:** a large improvement over bounce (BTC/BNB positive over 3 years, all three near break-even in the last year) but the success criterion is not met. Live defaults unchanged (bounce on the 15m layout: trend models need the 1h/1w roles, an env change). Next candidates: run this layout live in the signal log alongside (done: shadow log, v2.22.0, config `shadow`, shown in the dashboard Edge block), test 4h entry, exits with a trailing stop instead of a fixed target.
  - **Default decision (v2.24.0, 2026-11-04, by the project owner):** the closest layout becomes the default: `trend_pullback`, roles 1h / 4h / 1d / 1w, `trend.stop_atr` 3, `trend.target_r` 2, `backtest.days` 1095; the shadow log now runs `bounce` on 15m / 1h / 4h / 1d. Replay on v2.24.0 (3 years, fee 0.1%, split 0.7): BTC +0.078 R (452), BNB +0.038 (416), SOL −0.123 (323); out-of-sample BTC +0.060 (119), BNB −0.005 (117), SOL +0.007 (132). **The success criterion is still not met**: the dashboard keeps the unproven label and Level 7 stays blocked. Symbols with fewer than 200 weekly candles (XAUT) get `insufficient_data`.
  - **Fee correction (v3.1.1, 2026-11-04):** the earlier results used 0.1% as the round trip, but standard Binance spot is 0.1% per side. Same trades at 0.2%: BTC +0.025 R (95% bootstrap interval −0.10 to +0.15), BNB −0.011, SOL −0.154; out-of-sample BTC +0.002, BNB −0.063, SOL −0.030. Before any fee BTC is +0.132 R (+0.006 to +0.255). `backtest.fee_pct` is now 0.2; numbers above this line stay at the fee they were measured with. Slippage is still not modelled.
  - **Zone fix (v3.2.0, 2026-11-04, BTC, daily sample over one year):** on the 1w layout the top zone was 9.88% wide (median), 82.2% carried all four timeframes, top support and resistance overlapped in 87.7%, 56.6% read `compression_zone`, 14.7% of levels stood on the wrong side of the price. With `zones.max_width_percent` 2 and siding by the current price: 2.00%, 55.2%, 27.3%, 13.9%, 0%. `trend_pullback` trades are identical (3 years). `bounce` on 15m / 1h / 4h / 1d (one year, fee 0.2%) goes from −0.342 R (71 taken) to +0.029 R (36 taken): one year and few trades, not evidence of an edge.
  - **Fuller costs and trailing exit (v3.3.0, 2026-11-04, BTC only, 3 years, fee 0.2% + slippage 0.05%, next-open entry):** live layout −0.008 R (453 trades, 95% interval −0.133 to +0.124), out-of-sample −0.037, walk-forward −0.028 (2 of 6 periods positive). Trailing exit, `trail_atr` 2–6 × expiry 96–480: worse at 2–4 ATR (−0.09 to −0.01 R); the best in-sample set (6 ATR, 480 candles) goes from +0.212 R in-sample to −0.208 R out-of-sample, walk-forward over all sets −0.037. **Trailing does not add an edge; the exit stays fixed.** Longer expiry with the fixed exit: 480 candles +0.036 R, out-of-sample +0.007 (90 trades), interval not checked yet. Lead for a next test, seen after the fact on the whole period and so unproven: entry regime `trending` +0.216 R (183 trades) against `ranging` −0.159 R (270).
  - **Pullback as a condition (2026-11-04, BTC, 3 years, fee 0.2% + slippage 0.05%, next-open entry):** `trend_pullback` does not require a pullback: 1w and 1d trends alone give score 5; 268 of 452 taken trades had none. Those without: +0.011 R; with: −0.043 R, out-of-sample −0.257. A replay with the pullback required: −0.043 R (337 trades), out-of-sample −0.109, walk-forward −0.065. Not adopted. A higher score does not mean a better trade (with a pullback: score 5 +0.098 R, scores 6–9 −0.25 to −0.46 R, few trades).
  - **Against random entries (2026-11-04, BTC, 2,000 simulated runs, same plan, costs and volatility filters; entering at random moments while walking forward in time):** 1h: model before costs +0.123 R against +0.014 (random side), +0.063 (follow 1w), +0.069 (1w and 1d agree); after costs −0.011 R. Out-of-sample it does not clearly beat them, and 1w and 1d agree does better (+0.089 R, 48 trades). Average cost 0.134 R per trade; break-even cost about 0.23% whole period, 0.19% out-of-sample. These intervals only vary the entry moments on one price path, so they flatter the model; the simulation script is not in the repository.
  - **4h entry (2026-11-04, roles 4h / 1d / 1d / 1w, unchanged otherwise, costs 0.25%):** BTC +0.183 R (112 trades, 95% interval −0.065 to +0.435), out-of-sample +0.210 (26), walk-forward +0.152. Run unchanged on the other pairs: BNB +0.027 (116), out-of-sample −0.190 (27); SOL −0.124 (78, from 2024-06 for lack of 1w candles), out-of-sample +0.123 (25), no better than random. Cost falls to 0.04–0.06 R per trade. **The success criterion is not met and the live layout stays.** BTC alone may be its rising market (+117%) or chance.
  - **Decision (v4.0.1):** no more rule variants on this data for now. The shadow log moves from `bounce` 15m to `trend_pullback` 4h / 1d / 1d / 1w to collect new data: about 37 BTC trades a year, so more than a year before it means much. It logs every market in `engine.markets`, not only BTC.

## Success criteria

- Taken expectancy **> 0 R after a 0.1% fee on the out-of-sample 30%** for BTC, SOL and BNB, each with at least ±30 out-of-sample trades, using one shared parameter set.
- Until then the dashboard labels the signal as unproven.
- Level 7 (AI) starts only after this criterion is met.
